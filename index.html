StoryNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

StoryNest is a free storybook app for children, made by Rohit Bhoi ("we", "us"). This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. The short version: StoryNest does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information.

What StoryNest does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no sign-up.
  • It has no ads and no advertising partners.
  • It has no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not need the internet. All stories, pictures and sounds are stored inside the app.
  • It has no links that take a child out of the app.

What stays on your device

StoryNest remembers a few choices so it works the way you left it: which reading group is chosen (little or bigger kids), the reading speed, the text size, and which stories are marked as favorites. These are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes them.

Coloring pages are not saved or sent anywhere.

Reading aloud

"Read to me" uses the text-to-speech voice that is already installed on your phone. The voice, and how it works, is controlled by your phone and the voice app on it, not by StoryNest. StoryNest only hands it the story text on the screen. If your phone's voice app handles text in its own way, its own privacy policy applies to that part.

Children's privacy

StoryNest is designed for children aged 3 to 10 and for the grown-ups who read with them. Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to share and nothing for a parent to remove. If this ever changes, we will update this page and the app before it happens.

Stories and pictures

The stories are retold from old public-domain books and traditional tales. The "About us" page in the app names the sources. The pictures and sounds were made for StoryNest or come from sources released for free use.
